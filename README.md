# FinCoach 💰

FinCoach is an app that helps young adults make smarter financial decisions, and avoid costly mistakes before they happen.

## Who It's For

Young adults who are financially independent (or about to be) — think first apartment, first job, first credit card, first "wait, how do taxes work?" moment.

## What It Does

FinCoach combines learning, tracking, and simulation into one friendly experience:

- **Financial Life Simulation** — An interactive, animated life simulation that walks users through real-world scenarios. It collects info about their personal life, education, career, and finances, then generates a personalized future projection showing how their choices could affect their life and money over time.
- **Lessons** — Finance lessons that teach key money skills, with exercises that help the user understand the lessons better.
- **Finance Tracking** — Tools to help users keep an eye on their spending and financial health.
- **AI Finance Helper**- An AI Bot that helps users if they want to access support quickly and efficiently with any financial decisions
